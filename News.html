🐧 The Beginning of The Paper Game

The Paper Game Team has officially created Club Penguin Legacy: The Paper Game!

What started as a simple paper idea became a full penguin adventure with igloos, furniture, games, badges, coins, and events.

Players can now explore the world, play exciting games like JACKPOT!, Password Cracker, Penguin Maze Rush, and collect rewards.

This is only the beginning... More islands, updates, and surprises are coming soon!
